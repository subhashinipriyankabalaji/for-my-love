Writing a love letter... ✍️
Penning words with devotion
🏡✨
Asking about your well-being
📜🎶
Poetry pours out thinking of you
🤔💭
Words fail to express this depth
❤️
Oho...
💖✨
Forever in my heart
Kanmani Anbodu Kaathalan Naan Ezhuthum Kadithamae..
Dearest Kanmani, this is the letter written with love by your lover..
Ponmani Un Veetil Sowkiyama Naan Ingu Sowkyamae..
My precious one, are you fine at home? I am doing well here..
Unnai Enni Paarkaiyil Kavithai Kottuthu..
When I think of you, poetry flows effortlessly..
Athai Ezhutha Ninaikaiyil Vaarthai Muttuthu..
But when I try to write it down, words fail me..
